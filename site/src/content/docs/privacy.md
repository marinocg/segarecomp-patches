This site is a static documentation and catalog site hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no accounts, comments or server-side code of its own.

## Without your permission

* Nothing is requested from Google, and no analytics cookie is set. The pages, the search (which runs in your browser) and the catalog work fully.
* Your cookie choice itself is stored in your browser's local storage under the key `segarecomp-patches-consent-v1`. It records only whether you accepted analytics and when, and it never leaves your browser.
* GitHub, which hosts the site, processes the technical data of each request (such as your IP address) under [its own privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). This site does not receive or control that data.

## With your permission

If you choose **Accept analytics**, the site loads Google Tag Manager (container `GTM-WDL2MTLC`) from `googletagmanager.com`. Google can then receive your IP address and information about the pages you view, and the tags configured in that container may set cookies such as `_ga`. Only analytics storage is granted. Advertising storage, advertising user data and ad personalization stay denied, and no advertising or marketing integration is configured by this site.

The site does not decide by itself what the container does afterwards: the tags configured in it must also respect the consent state this site sets (Google Consent Mode v2). That configuration is the maintainer's responsibility.

## Changing or withdrawing your choice

Use **Cookie settings** in the footer of any page at any time. **Reject** after having accepted tells Google that analytics storage is denied, removes the analytics cookies this site's origin can see and reloads the page so that no container keeps running. Your choice is asked for again after a year.

A dedicated privacy contact and a legal entity name have not been published yet; until then use the repository's issue tracker for questions, and do not send personal data there.
