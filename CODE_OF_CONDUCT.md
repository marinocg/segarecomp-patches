# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standard

Be respectful, assume good faith and keep discussion about the work. Welcome people of every background and experience level.

Not acceptable: harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing someone's private information, and sustained disruption of review or discussion.

## Project-specific conduct

Asking for, offering or attaching commercial game images, firmware, extracted assets or links to where to get them is out of bounds in issues, pull requests and discussions (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [docs/publication-restrictions.md](docs/publication-restrictions.md)). Maintainers may remove such content without notice.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions and may restrict participation for conduct that breaks these rules. Report a concern privately through GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** form (Security tab) or by contacting the repository owner [@marinocg](https://github.com/marinocg). Reports are handled confidentially.
